# Purpose

This document defines the long-term AI strategy for the AI SVG Generator platform.

The objective is to introduce AI only where it creates measurable business value while preserving deterministic SVG generation for core workflows.

---

# AI Principles

## Principle 1

AI should enhance production workflows.

---

## Principle 2

AI should never compromise cutability.

---

## Principle 3

Deterministic geometry remains the source of truth.

---

## Principle 4

AI outputs must pass validation before export.
