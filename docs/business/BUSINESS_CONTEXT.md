# Business Overview

Etch 'N' Shine is a UK-based laser engraving and laser cutting business specialising in personalised gifts, custom products, event merchandise, and decorative items.

The business serves both B2C and B2B customers through:

* Shopify website
* Etsy store
* Direct business orders
* Corporate gifting opportunities

The business primarily uses laser technology to personalise and manufacture products from:

* Acrylic
* Mirror acrylic
* Plywood
* MDF
* Bamboo
* Slate
* Stainless steel
* Glass

---

# Business Vision

To become a highly efficient, AI-assisted personalised product business capable of producing professional laser-ready designs rapidly while maintaining exceptional product quality.

The business seeks to reduce manual design effort, improve consistency, and increase production capacity without increasing operational complexity.

---

# Current Business Challenges

## Challenge 1 – Manual Design Work

Many products require manual design preparation before production.

Examples include:

* Welding names together
* Creating cake toppers
* Adding support bridges
* Preparing SVG files
* Adjusting designs for specific materials

Current process often requires:

* LightBurn editing
* Vector manipulation
* Manual repair

This consumes significant time.

---

## Challenge 2 – SVG Sourcing

Finding commercially usable SVG artwork is often:

* Time consuming
* Expensive
* Inconsistent in quality

Many SVG files require significant modification before production.

---

## Challenge 3 – Design Consistency

Different fonts and designs behave differently when cut.

Common issues include:

* Floating letters
* Weak connections
* Unsupported geometry
* Broken decorative elements

These issues can lead to:

* Failed cuts
* Material waste
* Additional labour

---

## Challenge 4 – Scaling Production

As order volume grows, manual design preparation becomes a bottleneck.

The business requires automation to:

* Increase throughput
* Improve repeatability
* Reduce design time

---

# Project Purpose

The AI SVG Generator is intended to become the internal design platform used by Etch 'N' Shine to generate production-ready laser cutting files.

The platform will:

* Automate text welding
* Automate bridge creation
* Validate cut readiness
* Generate cake toppers
* Generate custom artwork
* Support future AI-assisted design workflows
